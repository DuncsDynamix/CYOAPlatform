How should the evidence record be laid out?

A4 pages in the Field manual style, using an example Doorstep record where one criterion couldn't be assessed (so the verdict is "Incomplete"; the honest states have to look good too). The record has its own URL and prints to PDF from the browser. Click one to select it.

A. Summary sheet + transcript appendix

Page 1 is everything a manager files: who, what, verdict, each criterion with a short quote. The full conversation follows as an appendix.

B. Criterion cards

Each criterion is a card with the learner's quoted words and the assessor's note. Decisions summarised after. Transcript via a link, not printed.
